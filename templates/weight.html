Understanding Body Mass Index (BMI) & Its Context

What BMI measures, how it is categorized, and why it is not a complete measure of health

📊 Standard BMI Categories

  • < 18.5: Underweight
  • 18.5 – 24.9: Normal / Reference Weight
  • 25.0 – 29.9: Overweight Range
  • ≥ 30.0: Obese Range

🩺 Important Limitations of BMI

BMI is a simple mathematical ratio based on height and weight. It does not distinguish between lean muscle mass and body fat. Athletes, weightlifters, and individuals with high bone density often register high BMIs while remaining exceptionally healthy.
